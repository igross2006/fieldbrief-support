Fieldbrief support

For help with your iPhone brief, include the airport identifier, the affected tab, and the report time. You can find the reporting station next to the observation.

Email Fieldbrief@gmail.com for support. You can also open a public issue. Please leave personal details out of public reports.

Looking up a small field

Tap Airport and enter its FAA identifier, such as 28A for Goose Creek. When a report comes from another airport, Fieldbrief displays the source and distance. A nearby observation describes conditions at that reporting station.

Reading the runway arrow

In Surface, open Runway winds and tap Show runway diagram. The arrow points toward the direction the reported wind comes from. For example, a 220° wind points toward the southwest on the north-up diagram. The card gives the headwind or tailwind and the crosswind for the selected runway end. Confirm the runway in use with current airport information.

Changing the appearance

Open Briefing guide and choose Light, Midnight, Graphite, or System. Briefing times use your device’s time zone. Raw aviation reports retain their original times.
