Fieldbrief Aviation Weather

Privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026. Ian Gross publishes Fieldbrief Aviation Weather for iPhone.

On your iPhone

Fieldbrief saves your bookmarked airports, last successful lookup, and appearance preference on your device. Remove a bookmark by tapping it again. Deleting the app removes its local settings. Fieldbrief does not ask for GPS access or require an account.

Weather requests

Your device contacts NOAA services and the Iowa Environmental Mesonet to retrieve weather. A request can include the airport identifier, the airport’s published coordinates, the weather product and forecast time. The providers receive your network IP address. NOAA states that its logs include the originating IP address, the requested resource and request time and may be kept indefinitely for security. Each provider manages its logs. Fieldbrief has no developer-operated weather server, advertising SDK, or usage analytics.

Purchases, links and support

Apple processes App Store purchases. Opening an external briefing site takes you to that provider. GitHub hosts these support pages and public issue reports. If you open a support issue, the information you post is public.
